Peanut Butter Bars

These peanut butter bars taste just like peanut butter cups. Rich layers of chocolate and peanut butter combine in this delicious, no-bake treat.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 1 cup butter or margarine, melted
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 ½ cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 4 tablespoons peanut butter
Steps
  1. Mix together the ingredients until well-blended.
  2. Press evenly into the bottom of a pan.
  3. Place chocolate chips and peanut butter in a bowl.
  4. Microwave on high, stirring every 15 seconds.
  5. Spread mixture over crust.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
