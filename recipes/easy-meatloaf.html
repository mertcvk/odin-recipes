Easy Meatloaf

Get back to basics with this easy meatloaf recipe. It's quick, traditional, delicious, and it's sure to please everyone at your table.

Ingredients
  • 1 ½ pounds ground beef
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup dried bread crumbs
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • ⅓ cup ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons prepared mustard
Steps
  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  3. Combine the ingredients, and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Stir ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard together in a small bowl until smooth, and spread evenly over the meatloaf.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven about 1 hour.
